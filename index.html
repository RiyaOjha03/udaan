Data provenance notes

Chemical coverage

Uranium-only baseline cannot support multivariate degradation analysis.
Coordinates locate sites. They do not establish flow direction, hydraulic connectivity, or downgradient status.

Available metadata

Coordinates, site class, and optional aquifer, formation, or well depth. Coverage varies by site.

Critical gaps

Water-level elevations, screen intervals, contemporaneous gradient/flow, connected aquifer geology, ISR-zone geometry, and operating/restoration phase.

Feature policy

No source distance, upgradient/downgradient, head gradient, or connectivity feature without verified source geometry and contemporaneous heads. Chemistry alone cannot resolve transport.

ISR degradation prediction: not evaluated. The public extract has no linked baseline/operations target, site phase, flow geometry, or paired chemistry.

Experimental component

Isolation Forest is gated on ≥3 real co-sampled chemistry features and ≥100 usable records. It scores relative chemical unusualness in the extract only.

No target, accuracy, AUC, R², RMSE, vulnerability class, or risk probability is asserted.

Spatial assessment

Grouped coordinate-block score stability is implemented. Without labels it is not predictive validation. Random CV risks leakage between nearby wells. Temporal validation awaits repeated records across meaningful phases.

Interpretation

Positive concentrations may be log-transformed; within-well change uses a prior observation only. Site ID/coordinates are excluded from chemistry predictors. Detector contamination is an operating assumption, not observed prevalence. Score is not degradation probability or source attribution.

Explanations

Percentile is explained by rank in observed values. If anomaly model fits, feature ablation measures score change when one feature is set to its training median. This is not causal evidence.

SHAP status

With optional dependencies and a fitted detector, Kernel SHAP can attribute its raw anomaly score. It does not explain degradation probability, health risk, or ISR impact. It is not run on the empirical percentile or an unfit detector. SHAP for a validated supervised target remains planned.

Research question & target

Can observations plus site monitoring support interpretable groundwater chemistry change and vulnerability assessment near hypothetical ISR?

Public baseline supports U reference screening and observed mapping, not degradation prediction. Candidate future target: within-well baseline-adjusted analyte change linked to documented phase and aquifer. Current labels are absent; no supervised target is asserted.
